Fiscal Drivers of Africa’s Sovereign Debt Crisis

In this project, I analyzed the fiscal and macroeconomic performance of many African economies using data compiled from various Central Banks and Statistical agencies of respective countries. In this analysis, I worked, cleaned, and explored 23,784 rows of records with 9 columns, including fiscal variables such as Budget Balance, Capital and Health Expenditure, GDP, Inflation, Unemployment, Government Debt, and Population metrics. I applied AI-driven anomaly detection and predictive modelling to find insights, uncover instability risks, and forecast economic trends through 2028. The project landed on a policy simulation based on three case scenarios, which resulted that to tame the fiscal crisis, the government should embrace optimal fiscal prudence by boosting revenue and cutting the cost of governance

Data-Driven Housing Price Prediction for Nigeria’s Real Estate Market

Addressing the challenge of accurately predicting residential property values in Nigeria, this project developed a robust machine learning–based pricing model using real-world housing data. I evaluated multiple algorithms through cross-validation and assessed performance using the Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE) metric. The XGBoost Regressor emerged as the best-performing model, achieving approximately 87% predictive accuracy. The project highlights my ability to apply advanced machine learning techniques to economic data, rigorously evaluate model performance, and translate analytical results into reliable, data-driven insights for real-estate decision-making.

Exploratory Analysis on the Economic Impact of Tourism

Investigating tourism’s role as a driver of economic growth, this project conducted a comprehensive exploratory and econometric analysis using a global dataset covering over 200 countries from 1999 to 2023. I performed extensive data cleaning and exploratory data analysis to uncover key relationships between tourism indicators and macroeconomic performance. Using Ordinary Least Squares (OLS) regression, the results revealed that tourism receipts, arrivals, exports, expenditures, and departures—alongside inflation and unemployment—are statistically significant determinants of economic growth. The project demonstrates my ability to translate large-scale economic data into evidence-based insights, apply rigorous statistical methods, and support data-driven policy and development decisions.
